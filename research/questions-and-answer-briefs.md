# Buyer questions and answer briefs

Status: editorial planning, not published page copy. These are not a collected Google People Also Ask report. Exact observed autocomplete strings are quoted and sourced. Other questions are clearly marked as editorial candidates. The answer direction is proposed plain-language copy, not a claim that an AI engine has cited it.

## 1. SEO

Primary destinations: `/seo-services`, `/seo-consultant-india` and the relevant genuine regional page.

1. **How long does SEO take to work?** Observed autocomplete, S08: `how long does seo take to work`.
   Suggested answer: SEO does not have a fixed completion date. Some technical fixes can be noticed sooner than improvements in competitive rankings. The starting condition of the site, competition, content quality and the work completed all affect progress. Agree on a baseline, priorities and a review schedule rather than a guaranteed ranking date.
2. **How long does SEO take for a new website?** Observed autocomplete, S08: `how long does seo take to work for a new website`.
   Cover crawlability, useful pages, discovery links and evidence. Do not invent a three-month promise or imply that a sitemap guarantees indexing.
3. **What is included in an SEO audit?** Editorial candidate.
   Explain access, crawling/indexing checks, page clarity, internal links and prioritised fixes. Distinguish the free initial review from a contracted implementation scope.
4. **How much does it cost to work with Fawaz BH?** Editorial candidate, first-party rate source F01.
   Existing Local SEO package starts at INR 14,999 per month. State its documented scope and link to `/seo-services#pricing-seo`; re-confirm rates with the owner before publishing new pricing claims. Do not represent one package as the cost of every SEO engagement.
5. **How do I choose an SEO consultant in India or Karnataka?** Editorial candidate.
   Ask for work relevant to the buyer, verifiable credentials, access/report ownership, clear fees, limits and an explanation of the work. Explain why a first-place guarantee is not evidence of competence. Link to Fawaz's actual case studies rather than a self-awarded leaderboard.

## 2. AEO

Primary destination: `/aeo-consultant`; integrated comparison destination: `/ai-seo-expert-india`.

1. **What does answer engine optimization mean?** Observed topic, S02: `answer engine optimization definition`.
   Suggested answer: AEO means making information easier for search and answer systems to find, understand and use when answering a question. That usually means clear answers, accurate business information and accessible pages. It overlaps with SEO and cannot guarantee a featured answer or citation.
2. **How is AEO different from SEO?** Observed autocomplete, S14: `aeo vs seo`.
   Explain that SEO addresses discovery in search results while AEO focuses on answering questions well. Both need useful, accessible and trustworthy information. They are not independent shortcuts.
3. **What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?** Observed comparison, S14: `aeo vs seo vs geo`.
   Compare outcomes, common foundations and reporting. Link to each discipline instead of repeating its entire page. Terminology overlaps across the industry; avoid treating one taxonomy as universal.
4. **Can you show an AEO example?** Observed topic, S02: `answer engine optimization examples`.
   Use an explicitly illustrative example of answering a price or eligibility question before describing detail. Do not present a made-up answer card as a real Google result.
5. **Does FAQ schema guarantee a place in answers?** Editorial candidate.
   No. Structured data should accurately describe visible content where relevant. It does not guarantee a rich result, a People Also Ask appearance or an AI citation. Useful FAQs should be written for buyers even when no rich result is available.

## 3. GEO

Primary destination: `/geo-consultant`.

1. **What does generative engine optimization mean?** Observed topic, S03: `generative engine optimization meaning`.
   Suggested answer: GEO is work intended to make a business and its information easier to discover and use in AI-generated answers. It includes clear business details, useful pages, reliable supporting evidence and checking how systems answer relevant questions. A business cannot control whether an engine recommends it.
2. **How can I get my business on ChatGPT?** Observed autocomplete, S09: `how to get my business on chatgpt`.
   Explain that there is no universal application that guarantees inclusion in organic ChatGPT recommendations. Help buyers distinguish a public website, a search citation, an app integration and paid advertising. Focus the service on accessible, accurate public information and genuine reputation.
3. **How can I get my business recommended on ChatGPT?** Observed autocomplete, S09: `how to get your business recommended on chatgpt`.
   Discuss question relevance, verifiable information and genuine third-party evidence without claiming to know hidden ranking weights. Avoid sending users to spam directories or purchasing fabricated reviews.
4. **How do you measure AI visibility?** Editorial candidate.
   Record a fixed set of relevant questions and the exact answer/citations from each engine on a schedule. Separate a brand mention from a link citation and a recommendation. Results vary across engines, dates, locations and sessions; a few successful prompts are not market share.
5. **Can a consultant guarantee that AI tools will recommend my business?** Editorial candidate.
   No. A consultant can improve information, accessibility and evidence, then measure observed changes. The engine decides what to display. Paid marketing is a different channel and should never be presented as an organic recommendation.

## 4. Performance marketing

Current destination: `/seo-services#pricing-ads`. Proposed primary service page: `/performance-marketing`, not created yet.

1. **Which is better, Google Ads or Facebook Ads?** Observed autocomplete, S12: `google ads vs facebook ads which is better`.
   Suggested answer: The better starting point depends on who you want to reach and how they buy. Search ads can reach people already looking for an offer. Meta ads can help people discover an offer while browsing social platforms. Compare qualified leads or sales against total costs rather than picking a winner from clicks alone.
2. **How do Google Ads and Facebook Ads costs compare?** Observed autocomplete, S12: `google ads vs facebook ads cost`.
   Explain auction variability, creative costs, landing pages and measurement. Do not quote universal CPCs or promise that one platform is always cheaper.
3. **Are advertising spend and your management fee the same thing?** Editorial candidate, F01.
   No. The existing single-platform rate card lists INR 11,999 per month as a management fee for spend under INR 30,000; media spend is paid separately to the platform. Keep both visible together and have the owner reconfirm the current rate before reuse.
4. **Should an ecommerce business use Google Ads or Meta Ads?** Observed topic, S12: `google ads vs facebook ads for ecommerce`.
   Cover search demand, product feed/creative readiness, margin, repeat purchase and attribution. A worked hypothetical is acceptable if labelled; a fictional ROAS case study is not.
5. **What do I need before paying for ads?** Editorial candidate.
   Cover a clear offer, a useful landing page, working enquiry/checkout flow, agreed budget, account access and consent-aware conversion measurement. Only promise specific implementation work after scope is agreed.

## 6. Digital marketing

Current destinations: `/seo-services` and `/seo-consultant-kodagu`. Proposed primary strategy page: `/digital-marketing-consultant`, not created yet.

1. **How much does digital marketing cost for a small business?** Observed autocomplete, S10: `how much does digital marketing cost for a small business`.
   Suggested answer: There is no single price for every business. Start with the customers you need to reach and the work required to reach them. SEO, social management and paid campaigns have different scopes. Advertising spend is separate from management fees, so compare the full cost rather than one headline number.
2. **Should I start with SEO, ads or social media?** Editorial candidate.
   Compare existing demand, urgency, available budget, website readiness, margins and the ability to handle enquiries. Recommend a focused starting point rather than sell all channels automatically.
3. **What does a digital marketing consultant do?** Editorial candidate based on S06's consultant intent.
   Explain goal-setting, channel selection, priorities and reviewing results. Distinguish strategy advice from execution work included in an agreed package.
4. **Should I hire an independent consultant or an agency?** Editorial candidate.
   Explain direct access and scope on one side, potential team capacity and specialist breadth on the other. Do not claim either model is universally better. Fawaz is an independent consultant, not an agency with invented departments.
5. **Can you work with businesses elsewhere in India or Asia?** Editorial candidate, F03 plus user goal.
   State the actual home base and documented remote delivery. Confirm fit, working language and any local requirements before agreeing to international work. Do not claim an Asia-wide team, office network or market-leading track record.
