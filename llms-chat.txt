## What the services involve

SEO addresses discovery and performance in traditional search. Technical SEO work includes Core Web Vitals optimisation for LCP, INP and CLS; JavaScript rendering and crawl-budget fixes; relevant structured data matching visible content and eligibility rules; canonical, hreflang and indexation cleanup; and XML sitemap, robots.txt and log-file analysis.

Local SEO includes Google Business Profile setup, optimisation and posts; Kannada and English bilingual keyword research; local citations and a name/address/phone consistency audit; Karnataka directory submissions and local backlinks; and city or neighbourhood landing pages. Local knowledge is strongest in Kodagu and Karnataka. Being geographically close is not presented as a substitute for technical work.

SEO content strategy includes topical-authority pillar and cluster mapping; long-form SEO articles of 1,500 to 3,500 words; service, location and comparison-page copy; strategic internal links and anchor planning; content refreshes; and keyword-cannibalisation fixes. This website includes AI-assisted writing and editing. Client drafting methods, review and approval responsibilities should be agreed in the scope rather than inferred from writing style.

LinkedIn and personal-brand SEO includes headline, About, skills and keyword optimisation; a personal-brand content calendar and thought leadership; Instagram, X and YouTube social SEO; personal-website SEO with Person schema and a sameAs entity graph; and digital PR and authority backlink outreach.

Answer Engine Optimisation, or AEO, makes information easier for an answer engine to retrieve and use as a direct answer. The specialist page distinguishes extracting a passage from synthesising a new answer. AEO work covers business identity and profile consistency, structured data checked against visible content and current eligibility, featured-snippet and People Also Ask opportunities, author biographies and credentials, supporting citations, and monthly AI-citation tracking. The service page names ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Copilot as tracked engines. These are service/measurement surfaces, not a claim that those engines endorse Fawaz.

The AEO page covers Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, featured snippets, People Also Ask, voice/direct-answer surfaces and other answer engines. It describes entity and structured-data foundations, answer-first content, per-engine measurement, and local AEO using language and Business Profile information. It offers AEO-only engagements scoped after a free audit as well as combined SEO/AEO packages. Third-party inclusion, a featured answer or a citation is not guaranteed.

Generative Engine Optimisation, or GEO, addresses how a business and its information appear in synthesised AI answers. The service page describes accessible content with clear headings, direct answers and useful detail; customer questions grouped by intent rather than duplicated into keyword pages; Google AI Overviews and AI Mode visibility review alongside other relevant engines; factual accuracy and source-quality review; and separate per-engine tracking of mentions, linked citations and explicit recommendations.

The GEO specialist page describes an AI-visibility audit and prompt-set baseline, entity resolution and structured data, answer-first architecture and cluster coverage, and brand-mention building with measurement. It distinguishes being mentioned from being cited and does not promise control over an AI engine's source selection. A prompt test is an observation under particular conditions, not a guarantee of the result every user will receive. GEO scope and pricing are discussed after the audit.

Digital marketing is described across the service and Kodagu pages as a combination of search, Google/Meta advertising, social media, web development and measurement. The published service catalogue includes social media marketing, performance marketing and website design alongside the search services. Their actual scope depends on the agreed package or quote.

Social media marketing covers content calendars, platform content, short-form video at the relevant tier, community management, paid-social support and reporting. Higher tiers include multi-platform management, influencer outreach coordination and full paid-social management. Advertising spend is separate from the service fee.

Performance marketing covers Google Ads, Meta Ads or both, depending on the tier. Published deliverables include campaign setup, targeting, optimisation, reporting, cross-platform strategy and A/B testing, conversion tracking, landing-page testing and a reporting dashboard at the relevant tier. Management fees and advertising budgets are separate.

Website design covers mobile-responsive business sites and custom sites. Depending on the package, work includes basic on-page SEO, a contact form, custom design, schema, a blog/CMS, speed optimisation, a custom design system, advanced interactions, multi-page architecture and third-party integrations. Website-design prices are one-time build prices, not monthly retainers.
